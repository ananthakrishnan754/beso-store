# BESO Website — Status, What's Built, and What's Pending

_Last updated: 2026-10-09_

## 1. Where the project stands (plain status)

- The storefront is **built and working**: 79 real BESO products, product
  pages, compare, AR ("View in Your Room"), a 3D product viewer, and a live
  home page.
- The **current home page is intentionally left as the client-approved
  structure**. We are not changing it while concepts are being reviewed.
- We have been building **switchable home-page concepts** (the client picks the
  direction; the live home stays untouched). The client has seen earlier
  concepts and called them "basic", so we are preparing **additional, more
  distinctive concepts** (including a two-mode "Office / Home" concept).
- **All media in the current build is interim.** The client told us he would
  supply the final videos and photos, so we generated temporary hero video /
  imagery to prove the design. Final media is still outstanding.

## 4. Why "basic" is hard to act on

"Basic" does not define a target. To move forward in a way the client is happy
with, we need **either** a selection from the concepts shown **or** specific
direction — e.g. a reference site he likes, the feeling he wants, must-have
sections, and what he dislikes about the current one. Section
[`REQUIREMENTS-NEEDED.md`](./REQUIREMENTS-NEEDED.md) lists the exact questions.
