PURPOSE OF THIS NOTE
This is background and talking points for your meeting with the client. We want
the meeting to move us forward quickly and keep the relationship positive.

WHERE WE ARE
- The storefront is built and working: 79 real BESO products, product pages,
  compare, AR ("View in Your Room"), a 3D product viewer, and a live home page.
- We built the home page to the structure and concept the client first agreed
  on. The client has since told us it feels "basic".
- We take that seriously. It is fair feedback, and it is also hard to act on
  because "basic" does not define a target. We are not defensive about it — we
  simply need a little more to aim at.
- All media currently on the site is interim. The client said he would send the
  final videos and photos, so we generated temporary media to prove the layout
  while we wait.

WHAT WE ARE DOING NOW
- We are preparing ADDITIONAL, more distinctive home-page concepts for the
  client to choose from. These will be ready in about 1-2 days.
- We are not touching the current live home page while this happens.

WHAT WE WOULD LIKE TO GET OUT OF THE MEETING
Either is fine — whichever the client prefers:
1. SELECT one of the concepts (current or the new ones), and we proceed to
   polish it; or
2. GIVE SPECIFIC DIRECTION, for example:
   - 2-3 reference websites whose feel he likes (any brand);
   - the feeling he wants (e.g. "expensive and calm", "bold", "warm", "tech");
   - what exactly feels basic in the current page (hero, type, spacing, images,
     colour, motion);
   - the must-have sections and anything to remove.
Concrete direction lets us match the quality he is expecting instead of
guessing.

CONTENT / ASSETS WE ARE WAITING ON
- Final photography (multi-angle per product) and the final videos he mentioned.
- The completed product sheet: the columns are right, but it currently has only
  3 sample products and most spec/photo cells are empty. We need every column
  filled for all products, and a defined set of photo angles.
- For 3D: whether the manufacturer already has CAD/3D files. If yes, that is the
  best and fastest route; if no, we model from dimensions + photos, with the
  honest caveat that photoreal 3D from single photos of upholstered/curved
  furniture is limited.

TONE WE WANT TO KEEP
- Respectful and collaborative. We are not blaming anyone; the earlier concept
  was agreed and delivered, and now the client wants a different direction —
  that is normal and we are happy to respond.
- Honest about trade-offs (e.g. "premium" depends on real media vs generated).
- Quality-first: we would rather do a few excellent concepts than many average
  ones.

SUGGESTED OPENING LINES FOR YOU
- "The team has the site working and is preparing a few more, more distinctive
  home-page options for you to choose from — they'll be ready in about 1-2 days."
- "To make sure the next round hits the mark, it would help to know a couple of
  sites whose look you like, and what specifically feels basic right now."
- "On the product sheet: the format is right, but it needs to be filled in for
  every product, and it would help to know if the manufacturers already have 3D
  files."

QUESTIONS TO BRING BACK TO US
- Which concept does the client prefer, or what is his concrete direction?
- Any reference links he likes?
- Does the client have final photos/videos ready, and a timeline?
- Do manufacturer CAD/3D files exist?
- Which sections are must-have vs optional?

We remain committed to getting this right and will keep the current site live
and stable in the meantime.
